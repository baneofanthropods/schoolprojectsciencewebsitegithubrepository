The Earth, Sun, and Moon system
-
The Earth, Sun, and Moon system is held together by gravity, which keeps each body in orbit. Earth orbits the Sun, and the Moon orbits Earth, which creates a 3 body system that creates predictable patterns such as Moon phases, tides, and eclipses. These formations shape many of the natural cycles experienced on our planet.

Movements
-
Earth spins on its axis, completing one revolution every 24 hours, which is called one day. This rotation causes day and night. This is because Earth spins and rotates; when one of the hemispheres is experiencing night, the other will be experiencing day. This is because only one of the hemispheres can be facing towards the sun at a time. Earth also completes one full revolution around the sun every 365 days, creating the seasons. The moon rotates on its axis and completes one full revolution around Earth in about 29.5 days. A rotation is a spin in a place on its axis, while a revolution is a movement around another object. These motions are controlled by gravity and determine how the Sun, Earth, and Moon align throughout the year.
