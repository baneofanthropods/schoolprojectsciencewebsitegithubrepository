What are the phases of the moon?
- Our Moon has a 29.5-day lunar cycle, which it strictly follows; this includes 4 primary and 4 intermediate phases that change as it orbits the Earth. The primary phases of the Moon consist of New Moon, First Quarter, Last Quarter, and Full Moon. The intermediate phases consist of Waxing Crescent, Waxing Gibbous, Waning Gibbous, and Waning Crescent. The order and occurrence of the Moon's faces goes New Moon at day 0, Waxing Cresent at days 1-6, First Quarter at day 7, Waxing Gibbous at day 8-13, Full Moon at days 14-15, Waning Gibbous at days 16-21, Last(third) Quarter at day 22, Waning Cresent at days 23-29, and Back to a New Moon at day 29.5. This is the strict Lunar cycle that the Moon follows.
